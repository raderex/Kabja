Privacy Policy

KTM School Bus Tracker  |  Last updated: January 2025

KTM School Bus Tracker ("we", "our", "app") is committed to protecting the privacy of students, parents, and drivers who use our service in the Kathmandu Valley.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location data (Drivers only): Precise GPS coordinates, speed, and bearing are collected in real time while the Driver app is running (including in the background). This data is used exclusively to display the bus position to subscribed parents on the same route.
  • Account information: Username and hashed password to authenticate driver and parent roles.
  • Route subscriptions: Which bus route(s) a parent account has linked, stored on our server to filter WebSocket updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Live bus position is broadcast in real time only to parents registered on the same route. It is not shared with any third party.
  • Location data in Redis expires automatically after 30 seconds if no new ping arrives. We do not build or store a historical journey log beyond the current school trip.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade personal data.

3. Background Location (Driver App only)

The Driver App requests "Always On" (background) location permission solely to continue transmitting the bus's GPS position while the screen is off or the app is minimised. This is a core function of the service. The Parent App does not request background location.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/WSS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Location data is held in Redis with a 30-second automatic expiry.
  • Authentication tokens use HS256 JWT with a 24-hour expiry.
  • The server is hosted within Nepal/Asia-Pacific region and does not transfer data to third-country processors.

5. Children's Privacy

This app does not collect data directly from children. Location data belongs to the driver (an adult). We comply with applicable child privacy regulations in Nepal.

6. Third-Party Services

  • OpenFreeMap / Protomaps: Map tiles are fetched from tiles.openfreemap.org. No user-identifying data is sent; standard CDN logs (IP, user-agent) apply per their privacy policy.
  • Nominatim (OSM): Address search queries are proxied through our server. Search strings may include place names typed by the user.
  • Google: Not used. We have zero dependency on Google Maps, Google Analytics, Firebase, or any other Google service.

7. Data Retention

Live location data: 30 seconds. Account data: retained while the account is active and deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us at the address below. Driver location history is not retained beyond the current school trip, so there is nothing to retrieve.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, email: privacy@yourdomain.com
