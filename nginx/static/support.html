Frequently Asked Questions

The bus isn't showing on the map Parent App

The bus marker appears only when the driver has started tracking and the device has a GPS signal. If school hasn't started yet or the driver hasn't opened the Driver App, no marker will show. Try pulling down to refresh. If the bus was recently active and disappeared, it may have lost signal temporarily.

ETA shows "—" instead of a time Parent App

ETA is calculated based on live routing data. It appears after the first GPS ping is received and a route to the school has been computed. Allow 10–15 seconds after the bus appears on the map.

Background tracking stopped on iOS Driver App

iOS may suspend background apps to save battery. To prevent this: go to Settings → General → Background App Refresh and enable it for KTM Bus Driver. Also ensure Location is set to Always under Settings → Privacy → Location Services → KTM Bus Driver.

Background tracking stopped on Android Driver App

Some Android manufacturers (Xiaomi, OPPO, Huawei) aggressively kill background apps. Go to Settings → Battery → App battery management and set KTM Bus Driver to "No restrictions". Also disable battery optimisation for the app.

How do I set up a new route?

Route setup requires admin access. Please contact your school's transport administrator. They can create routes and assign buses via the Admin Panel.

Is my child's location tracked?

No. Only the bus driver's device location is tracked. The app does not collect any data from children or student devices. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
